Method & limits

How this desk works

01

Google's public index

Each keyword is sent to Google News as a site:instagram.com query inside your time window. Google's signed article links are resolved to the original public Instagram URL.

02

Public post previews

Resolved posts are queried through Instagram's public oEmbed endpoint for author, caption and preview image. Private or deleted posts drop out. No login or cookie is used.

03

Optional Apify read

If connected, an Apify actor adds public metrics and exact timestamps. The run has a hard per-run cost cap and a cooldown. Google remains the default free source.

04

Relevance, not hype

Posts are scored on freshness, exact keywords, keyword overlap, question/advice language, public engagement and promo penalties. High/Low relevance is Keyword Deck's editorial heuristic, not an Instagram ranking.

Freshness is an index timestamp. Google News usually reports when it found the post. Without Apify, the exact Instagram publish time may be earlier. The UI says “indexed” for that reason.

No automated engagement. Keyword Deck never likes, follows, comments, messages or scrapes private data. You open the post and decide. Respect each platform's terms and each community's rules.

Manual lanes. Reddit, Facebook, Threads and LinkedIn open a keyword search in your default browser. Keyword Deck does not read those pages or use your accounts.

Local by design. The app listens only on 127.0.0.1. Searches, tokens and history stay in the local data folder.
